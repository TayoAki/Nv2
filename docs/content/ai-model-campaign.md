# AI model content plan: Nyoni Couture

Short videos of a consistent AI menswear model wearing real Nyoni pieces, posted on Instagram Reels and TikTok. The bio links to nyonicouture.com and the app.

The production method is Wholesale Ted's "AI influencer" workflow, minus Amazon:
1. Make a still frame with Nano Banana Pro.
2. Swap outfits in the same pose.
3. Turn each frame into a 3–6 second clip with Omni.
4. Cut the clips together in CapCut.
5. Optionally change the voice with ElevenLabs.

**Researched:** 6 October 2026 with ScrapeCreators. That's 351 TikTok videos and 75 Instagram Reels across 12 menswear searches, plus the top comments on six of the best performers.

**What they all do:**
- **One AI model** (or a "digital fashion duo"), the same face every time.
- **Same pose, same backdrop,** with only the outfit changing. Each frame shows one colour combination or rule.
- **Text on screen,** music, no talking. Hooks like "Save these colour combinations for later 🤍" and "Which one would you try?"
- **Optimised for saves:** 100K–480K saves per hit. Saves are what push these videos.
- **Their single biggest format is couples:** "COUPLE OUTFIT inspiration to look good together" (14.4M), "Couple colour combinations" (4.4M). A man and a woman, coordinated colours.

**Why this suits Nyoni better than the Amazon version:** @layrandlayr is a clothing *brand* that used this exact format to reach 1M followers and sell its own pieces. Nyoni can do the same with its own suits and blazers, and keep the full margin instead of a 1–4% affiliate commission.

**What this changes in the plan:**
- Script 2 ("Which combo looks most premium?") is the core format. Post it most often.
- Add a **couple colour-combination** series. He wears Nyoni; she wears simple neutral pieces that coordinate. It's the trend's top format, and it reaches the women who buy for, or influence, men (section 2).

## 2. ICP: who to make this for

### What the comments show
- **Women are a large share of the menswear audience,** and they buy for, or steer, the men in their lives:
  - "Taking notes to make sure my teen son has a top notch closet" (1,980 likes);
  - "I wish I had the money to play dressup with my husband";
  - "I've already sent him all the men's videos you've made";
  - "Who is the female creator version of you?" (1,422 likes).
- **Women creators get the highest engagement on menswear** (@anastasiia.preston at 17.9%, @siennarae.muse, @whatmyboyfriendwore).
- **On a menswear stylist's video:** "you need to create an app!" (902 likes). That's the Nyoni app.

### Recommendation: one man as the face, with a woman as a recurring second voice

**Primary ICP (the buyer and wearer).** A man aged 25–45 with an occasion coming up: a wedding (as guest or groom's party), a promotion or new job, a gala, date nights. He knows he should own a good navy or charcoal suit but doesn't know how to style it or what to buy first. Spends $500–$1,000 on a piece he'll keep for years.

**Secondary ICP (the influencer and gift-buyer).** His partner, wife or mother. She saves styling videos, sends them to him, and often makes the decision. Wedding season and the holidays are her moments.

**The cast**
- **"The Nyoni Man"**: one consistent AI male model, used in every video. The clothes are menswear, so the face of the brand is a man. Keep him the same person every time (same face, build and setting style). Consistency is how the AI accounts build recognition.
- **"Her"**: a recurring female AI character in about **1 in 4 videos**, never as a second wearer of Nyoni product. She appears in couple formats: "styling him for a wedding", "rate his fit", "his and hers wedding-guest colours". This reaches the secondary ICP, which the data says is large and highly engaged.
- **Don't start with two equal influencers.** It splits recognition, and Nyoni doesn't sell womenswear.

## 5. Scripts

Each script lists the **frames** (one Nano Banana Pro image each, same pose and setting, outfit swapped), the **clip prompt** for Omni (camera fixed, 4 seconds each), the **voiceover**, the **on-screen text**, and the **caption**. Voiceover lines are timed to about 2 seconds of speech per frame.

### Script 1: "5 suits every man needs" (strongest proven format)
- **Setting:** warm loft, full body, facing the camera.
- **Hook text:** "5 suits every man needs (in this order)."
- **Frames and voiceover:**
  1. Midnight Navy Three Piece Suit, white shirt, Antwerp boots: *"Number one: navy. Weddings, interviews, dinners. It does everything."*
  2. Charcoal Three Piece Suit, Chelsea II boots: *"Two: charcoal. The serious one. Boardrooms and funerals."*
  3. Evano Windowpane (grey): *"Three: a pattern. Windowpane says you know what you're doing."*
  4. Dove Gray Herringbone Double Breast: *"Four: double-breasted, for when you want to be remembered."*
  5. Sovereign Black Double-Breasted Tuxedo: *"Five: the tux. Because black tie always comes."*
  6. Back to navy, he buttons the jacket: *"Start with one. Save this for later."*
- **Omni clip prompt (each frame):** "Camera fixed. He makes a small confident adjustment to the jacket, buttons it, and looks at the camera. Natural, subtle movement. No zoom."
- **Caption:** "5 suits every man needs, in the order to buy them. Which one are you starting with? 👇 All five at nyonicouture.com (link in bio). #mensfashion #suits #menswear #mensstyle"

### Script 2: "Which combo looks most premium?" (saves magnet)

- **Hook text:** "Which looks most expensive? 1, 2, 3 or 4?"

## 6. Production checklist for each video

1. **Frames:** in Nano Banana Pro, attach the Nyoni Man reference and the store product photos. Prompt: "same man, same pose and setting as the reference, now wearing exactly these pieces." Use 9:16, and make 3–4 options per frame.
2. **Check every frame against the real product:** lapels, buttons, pattern, colour, pocket square fold. **Reject any frame where the garment changed.** You're selling that exact piece.
3. **Clips:** Omni, frames mode, 4 seconds, 720p. Prompt: "Camera fixed…" plus the movement or the line he says.
4. **Edit:** in CapCut, upscale to 1080p, trim the silences, add the on-screen text and a trending sound. Keep the total at **15–35 seconds**.
5. **Voice (optional):** in ElevenLabs, pick one voice for him and keep it forever.
6. **Post:** turn on the AI-generated label (Instagram and TikTok both have one). Bio link: nyonicouture.com, or a short link page with the products.
7. **Measure:** saves and shares matter more than likes. Double down on whichever format gets the most saves per view.

**Cadence:** 4–5 videos a week. Rotate the six scripts, then remake the top two with new products.

- **Bio link:** "Try any of these on yourself: Nyoni app." The try-on, the body scan ("find your size in 30 seconds") and the stylist are natural follow-ups for viewers who ask "would this suit me?".
